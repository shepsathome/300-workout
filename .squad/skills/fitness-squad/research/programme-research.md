## Simon Waterson — James Bond (Daniel Craig)

### Background
Former Royal Marine Commando. Craig's trainer since 2006 (*Casino Royale* through *No Time to Die*). Also trained Chris Pratt. Published "Commando Fitness Programme."

### Core Philosophy (Direct quotes from simonwaterson.com)

> *"Intensity is the vital ingredient to getting a gaged chemical response from the body & the brain. Create intensity & the body's natural reaction is to adapt."*

> *"One theory does not fit all, but a large number of contributing factors do play a vital role in every theory."*

**The Three Ds:** *Dedication, Discipline, Design* — "one doesn't work without the other."

**Honesty about genetics:**
> *"Its up to us to figure some parts of this out ie food group type (protein or carb) body type, also our responsive tissue type."*

### System Architecture (Team-based)
1. **Sports Scientist** — measures, records, logs human bio-patterns
2. **Individual Programme Design** — data-driven, not generic
3. **Elite Sports Chef** — weekly menu, clinically calculated calories/macros
4. **Neutroceutical/Supplement Delivery** — sleep, recovery, stress management
5. **Recovery Experts** — sports therapist and physio

### Training Approach — Bond Transformation
- **Military-style circuits** — not bodybuilder splits, total-body functional fitness
- **Compound movements** — pull-ups, deadlifts, bench press, overhead press, squats
- **Cardiovascular conditioning** — rowing, boxing, swimming (integral, not bolt-on)
- **Morning sessions** — Craig trained at 6am before filming
- **Location-independent** — hotel gyms, beaches, wherever Bond was shooting
- **Phase structure**: hypertrophy → strength → conditioning → maintaining on set

### Bond Physique Target
"Fighting fit" not "beach body" — broad shoulders, functional upper body, visible abs from low body fat. Kept legs athletic, not bulky.

### Recovery
Physio, sports massage, quality sleep, "powerful naps," neutroceuticals.
> *"Without rest your body cannot adapt."*
> *"Over training is very common when people start out or push their current regime. Good massage, good sleep, powerful naps, & nutroceuticals."*

## Magnus Lygdback — Tarzan (Skarsgård), Batman (Affleck), Wonder Woman (Gadot)

### The Lygdback Phase Model (from "The Magnus Method")
- **Phase 1: Foundation** (4-6 weeks) — movement patterns, mobility, moderate load
- **Phase 2: Hypertrophy** (6-8 weeks) — volume focus, 8-12 reps
- **Phase 3: Strength/Power** (4-6 weeks) — lower reps, heavier loads
- **Phase 4: Functional/Conditioning** — metabolic circuits

### Key Principles
- **Compound first, isolation after** — every session starts with primary compound lift
- **Full ROM + Control** — controlled eccentrics (3-4s lowering), pauses at bottom, explosive concentric
- **Progressive overload — systematic** — small weight increases weekly, or rep progression
- **48-hour muscle group recovery** minimum

### Nutrition
- 2-2.2g protein per kg bodyweight
- "Meal prep is non-negotiable"
- 5 meals/day, regularly spaced
- Carb cycling: higher on training days, lower on rest
- Minimum 3L water/day
- "Food is fuel, not reward or punishment"

## David Kingsbury — Wolverine (Jackman), Gyllenhaal, Fassbender

### Core Principles (All from verified blog posts)

**Progressive overload:**
> *"Train with weights for 3-4 times per week aiming to work each muscle twice per week and to apply overload when possible."*

**Calorie deficit for fat loss:**
> *"Eat fewer calories than you burn. Everything else is interesting and can help you towards your goal, but nothing breaks this rule."*

**Train for the outcome, manage calories for fat loss:**
> *"I always encourage people to train for things like — improved health, strength, fitness, muscle gain or maintenance, better movement, enjoyment — and then manage calorie intake for fat loss."*

**Phase-based training:**
> *"I'm not talking about a dirty bulk and starvation shred, instead I would focus on phases of building lean muscle and phases of dropping fat whilst maintaining muscle."*

### Home Gym Adaptation (from his blog)
Four strategies when limited equipment:
1. **Creativity** — *"If you only have 10kg dumbbells then 4×8 bench press might not cut it. Find new ways to challenge yourself."*
2. **Change angles** — incline, decline, different grips change stimulus dramatically
3. **Tempo manipulation** — slow eccentrics (3-4s down) are as effective as heavier weights for hypertrophy
4. **Add bands** — *"Bands are a fantastic cheap way of adding variety and new movements"*
5. **German Volume Training (10×10)** — *"can be a really effective way of making training more effective"*

### Wolverine Wave-Loading
- Progressive overload each week
- Variety of rep ranges: 5×5 strength, 4×12 hypertrophy, 3×20 metabolic
- Reverse pyramid training (heaviest first, reduce weight + increase reps)
- Calorie cycling: high on training days, lower on rest

### Key Mistake Warnings
- *"Don't try and out train a loose diet"*
- *"Small adjustments to keep on track. Don't chop and change too much"*
- *"The compound effect of small changes over time beats huge one off changes every time"*

## Synthesis: What This Gives the Programme Architect

**Phase model** — Waterson's hypertrophy → strength → conditioning + Lygdback's 4-phase structure = the periodisation framework for Adam's programme.

**Home gym mastery** — Kingsbury's tempo manipulation, angle changes, bands, and GVT specifically address Adam's equipment constraints (max 15kg DBs).

**Progression protocol** — Kingsbury's wave-loading + Lygdback's systematic overload = clear rules for when to increase weight, reps, or change phase.

**Non-negotiable** — the programme must have distinct phases, not endless repetition of the same week.
