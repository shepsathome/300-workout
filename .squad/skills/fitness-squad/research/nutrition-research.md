## 3. FRAMEWORK: FLEXIBLE DIETING (IIFYM)

Norton's core hierarchy:
1. **Total calories are king** — energy balance determines weight change
2. **Macros are second** — protein is non-negotiable; carbs and fats can flex
3. **Food quality matters for satiety, micronutrients, gut health** — not metabolic magic
4. **Adherence > perfection** — flexible dietary control outperforms rigid restriction long-term

> *"If It Fits Your Macros is a valid philosophy, but it's not a license to eat exclusively junk food."*

**Practical rule:** 80-90% whole/minimally processed foods; remaining 10-20% can be whatever fits macros.

## 4. FRAMEWORK: LEUCINE THRESHOLD & PROTEIN DISTRIBUTION

### The Mechanism (Norton's primary scientific contribution)
1. Protein consumption raises plasma leucine
2. Leucine activates **mTOR** in skeletal muscle
3. mTOR phosphorylates **p70 S6K** and **4E-BP1** — translation initiation factors
4. **Leucine threshold: ~3g per meal (~0.05g/kg BW)** — below this, MPS is sub-maximal
5. MPS peaks 45-90 min post-meal, returns to baseline by 180 min ("refractory MPS")

### Leucine Content by Source

| Protein Source | Leucine % (w/w) | MPS Response |
|---|---|---|
| Whey | ~10.9% | ✅ Strong |
| Egg | ~8.8% | ✅ Strong |
| Soy | ~8.0% | ❌ Below threshold at typical serving |
| Wheat/Gluten | ~6.8% | ❌ Below threshold at typical serving |

### Per-Meal Distribution (Norton et al. 2017)
- Balanced distribution across meals → **30-45% greater MPS** vs. skewed
- Optimal: **3-5 meals, each 30-40g quality protein, spaced 3-4 hours apart**
- Too many tiny meals = each below leucine threshold
- Too few meals = missed MPS stimulation windows

### For Adam (80kg)
- **Per-meal target: ~35-42g across 4 meals** = hits leucine threshold 4x/day
- At 130-150g/day = 1.625-1.875 g/kg — sweet spot for recomp
- Pushing to **160g (2.0 g/kg)** would be safer for muscle retention during deficit

## 5. FRAMEWORK: METABOLIC ADAPTATION & ADAPTIVE THERMOGENESIS

**Source: Trexler/Norton 2014 (PMID: 24571926)**

### What Happens During a Cut

| TDEE Component | Change | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **BMR/REE** | ↓ Beyond mass loss prediction | ↓ T3, ↓ leptin |
| **NEAT** | ↓ Significantly | Hypothalamic energy-conservation response |
| **EAT** | ↓ More efficient at same workload | Persistent hypothyroidism |
| **TEF** | ↓ Less food = less thermic effect | Absolute magnitude falls |

### Key Stats
- Adaptive thermogenesis = ~15% of TDEE reduction beyond what weight loss explains
- Previously-dieted individuals: **18% lower TDEE** than never-dieters at same weight
- **71% of that difference from non-resting components** (NEAT + EAT)
- Adaptations **persist** after active weight loss ends

### Practical Recommendations
- Use the **smallest possible deficit** that yields appreciable weight loss
- View weight reduction as a **stepwise process**
- **Diet breaks every 6-8 weeks** (maintenance for 1-2 weeks) — attenuates adaptive thermogenesis

## 6. FRAMEWORK: REVERSE DIETING

**Source: Trexler/Norton 2014; Norton's *Complete Reverse Dieting Guide***

### Protocol
- Add **~50-100 kcal per week** (mostly from carbohydrates)
- Track weight weekly — if stable/dropping, add another increment
- Continue until reaching maintenance or desired intake
- Duration: 6-16+ weeks depending on deficit severity

### Why It Matters
- Prevents fat overshoot after restrictive phases
- Normalizes leptin, T3, testosterone
- Mitigates adipocyte hyperplasia during weight regain

## 7. FRAMEWORK: NEAT (Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis)

**Source: Trexler/Norton 2014; Peos/Norton 2019**

### Key Insights
- NEAT can differ by **2,000 kcal/day** between similar-sized individuals
- Structured cardio (~200-400 kcal) often causes compensatory NEAT reduction
- Increasing daily step count is **non-compensatory** for most people
- **Target: 8,000-10,000+ steps/day** before adding formal cardio
- Step tracking is a better fat loss indicator than formal cardio tracking

## 10. RECOVERY NUTRITION

### Post-Workout
- **20-40g quality protein within 0-2 hours** post-training
- Whey ideal: rapid digestion, highest leucine
- Anabolic window is ~2 hours, not 30 minutes

### Pre-Sleep (ISSN recommendation)
- **30-40g casein before bed** → increases overnight MPS without affecting lipolysis
- Slow digestion sustains amino acid availability during overnight fast

### Carb Timing
- **Pre-workout: ~40-60g carbs 30-60 min before** strength sessions (fuels performance)
- Post-workout carbs: not urgent unless training twice/day
- Leucine/carb supplement ~2h after meal **extends MPS duration** (Wilson/Norton 2011)

## 11. INTERMITTENT FASTING (16:8 TRE) — ADAM'S PROTOCOL

Adam follows a 16:8 intermittent fasting protocol (Mon–Fri), with an eating
window of approximately 10am–6pm. This section integrates the IF evidence
into Layne's nutrition framework.

### Protocol Compatibility

16:8 IF is **compatible with the Spartan-Spider Programme** per current
evidence (15+ studies, 2016–2025). Key findings:

- **Fat loss with lean mass preservation** when combined with RT
  (Ashtary-Larky et al., 2021 meta-analysis: −1.36kg fat, NS lean mass loss)
- **Improved metabolic health markers** — insulin sensitivity, HOMA-IR,
  inflammatory markers (Moro et al., 2021, 12-month RCT)
- **Cardiovascular benefits** relevant at age 45 — ↓LDL, ↑HDL, ↓IL-6

### IF-Aware Nutrition Rules (Layne's Position)

1. **Per-meal protein** is MORE important under IF — each of the 3-4 meals
   within the 8h window MUST hit the leucine threshold (30-40g protein).
   Norton et al. 2017 showed 30-45% greater MPS with balanced distribution.

2. **3-4 feeding events in 8 hours** is achievable with 2.5-3h spacing.
   The "muscle full" effect resets within 90-180 min, so this spacing is
   physiologically sufficient for distinct MPS pulses.

3. **Do NOT flag "no dinner" or "only 2 meals"** when IF is enabled and
   all feeding events fall within the designated window. The analysis should
   instead verify:
   - Total daily protein ≥ target (152g)
   - Number of meals with ≥30g protein (target: 3-4)
   - All meals within the designated window
   - Total calories meeting surplus/recomp target

4. **Caloric under-eating risk**: IF naturally suppresses appetite (Moro
   2021). Adam must actively track and eat to target — don't let IF become
   an accidental cut.

5. **Hormonal monitoring**: Testosterone and IGF-1 may decline with long-
   term IF (Moro 2016, 2021). If Adam reports persistent low energy,
   reduced libido, or training performance decline beyond 8 weeks, flag
   to Nguyen for recovery assessment and consider narrowing the fast.

### Fasting Window Analysis

When reviewing nutrition data with IF enabled:
- Classify meals as "within window" or "outside window"
- A meal at 9:50am is acceptable (close to window open)
- Post-workout nutrition is the #1 priority — break fast within 60-90min of training
- WillPowders bone broth shake with milk BREAKS the fast (contains calories + protein)

### Creatine Supplementation Meta-Analysis — Frontiers in Nutrition, April 2026
**Source:** [Frontiers in Nutrition](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2026.1800546/full)
**Persona relevance:** Layne (Nutrition Strategist)

**Key insights:**
- Systematic review and meta-analysis pooling 37 RCTs in men aged 18–30
- Creatine supplementation led to significant gains in fat-free mass (FFM) and lean body mass (LBM) when combined with resistance training
- Improvements in both upper and lower body strength and peak power output
- Effects were much smaller or absent without concurrent resistance training — reinforces that creatine is a training amplifier, not a standalone supplement
- Emerging research on cognitive and neuroprotective benefits of creatine beyond muscle

**New frameworks/techniques:**
- Creatine's role expanding from pure sports performance to broader health applications (brain health, metabolic disease, heart failure)
- Standard dosing confirmed: 3-5g/day constant dose equally effective as loading protocol

**Conflict with existing material:** None — reinforces Norton's existing recommendation in Signature Move #6 ("Creatine. 5g per day. Every day. Just take it.")
