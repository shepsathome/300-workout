## Mark Twight — Gym Jones / 300

### Background
Mark Twight (born 1961) — world-class extreme alpinist who survived routes with "50% chance of survival." Founded Gym Jones in Salt Lake City ~2003. In 2005, trained 35 actors and stuntmen for *300*. Subsequently trained casts for *Man of Steel*, *300: Rise of an Empire*, *Batman v Superman*, *Wonder Woman*, *Justice League*, *Aquaman*.

### Core Philosophy (Verified direct quotes)

**The North Star:**
> *"Appearance is the consequence of fitness, and confidence is a consequence of capability."*
— Vanity Fair

**Mental first:**
> *"The muscle we are interested in training is inside the skull."*
— Outside Online

> *"If the mind is not first trained to enjoy hard work, to relish suffering, to address the unknown, then no program can be effective."*
— Outside Online

**Smart over hard:**
> *"It's easy to be hard, but it's hard to be smart."*
— Old Marine saying Twight adopted

**Strength is the glass:**
> *"Strength is the glass. All your other training is the liquid."* (Quoting Dan John)

### The 300 Method (Vanity Fair, verified)
*"Instead of adding muscle to the men's frames as a bodybuilder might — through programs of progressive overload using exercises that isolate each muscle group — Twight stripped their bodies of fat while teaching them to move with a more natural athleticism, using training techniques that he'd refined while working with military special forces. Twight put the men through high-intensity interval programs of compound movements — such as clean, press, and snatch using barbells and kettlebells, which engages both large and small muscle groups from the feet all the way up through the legs, glutes, chest, back, shoulders, arms, and hands."*

### The Actual 300 Workout (nerdfitness.com, verified)
300 total reps, performed in sequence for time with no rest:
1. Pull-ups — 25 reps
2. Deadlifts (135 lbs / ~61kg) — 50 reps
3. Push-ups — 50 reps
4. 24-inch Box Jumps — 50 reps
5. Floor Wipers (barbell overhead, raise legs side to side) — 50 reps
6. Single-arm Kettlebell Clean & Press (36 lbs / ~16kg) — 50 reps (25 each arm)
7. Pull-ups — 25 reps

### Key Principles (Outside Online deep-dive)

**1. Periodisation — not random workouts:**
> *"The biggest disappointment with CrossFit came from treating training as competition… Our intensity declined, form degraded. Now we build in recovery periods to assure proper intensity and form. We insist on progressively harder intervals (the last should be the fastest). We don't train to failure. We mimic work/rest intervals common to the sport-specific task."*

**2. Foundation then specificity:**
> *"The gym is not our sport. Everyone here trains for something else."*

**3. No training to failure:**
Explicitly stated. The last interval should be the fastest. Quality over collapse.

### Signature Protocols
- **Tail Pipe**: 3 rounds partner work — Row 250m, hold 2× 53lb KBs in front rack while partner rows
- **30-30s**: 30 seconds reps, 30 seconds hold at lockout × 4 sets
- **Jones Crawl**: 3 sets of deadlift at 115% BW × 10 + 25 box jumps, timed
- **Tabata-style intervals** (influenced by Dr. Izumi Tabata's research)
- **Concept2 Rowing Machine** — a Gym Jones staple

### Mistakes Twight Warns Against
- Training without a plan: *"We weren't making progress because we weren't planning"*
- Using the stopwatch as competition: *"Treating training as competition — intensity declined, form degraded"*
- Isolating muscles: *"Instead of isolating each muscle group"*
- Training to failure regularly: *"We don't train to failure"*
- Ego-lifting: Men need liberating from *"greed for the power of the number on the end of the weight"*

### Nutrition
Zone-like strategy: roughly equal parts carbs, protein, and fat. *"More protein, more fiber, more healthy fats like fish oil; way fewer processed carbohydrates."*
> *"Want to lose weight fast? Try not drinking alcohol for a month."*

### Mental Training Environment
- No mirrors, no TV in the gym
- High-stimulus environment (punk rock)
- Psychological "re-mapping" is as important as physical training
- Social pressure and shared suffering build commitment

## George Ashwell — Tom Holland, Spider-Man

### Background
CrossFit Level 1 certified trainer, UK-based. Trained Holland from *Spider-Man: Homecoming* (2017) through subsequent Marvel films. Built on Holland's gymnastics/ballet background (Billy Elliot stage show).

### Core Philosophy
**Functional athleticism over aesthetics.** CrossFit-influenced but tailored — make Holland *move* like Spider-Man, not just look like one.

### Target Physique
5'8", ~160 lbs, visible muscle in a lean frame. Emphasis on **relative strength** (strength-to-weight ratio) not absolute mass.

### Training Pillars
1. **Gymnastics & Ring Work** — Pull-up rings, muscle-ups, dips, L-sits
2. **Olympic Lifting Basics** — KB swings, cleans, snatches (lighter, high-rep, conditioning)
3. **Bodyweight Circuits** — AMRAP and EMOM formats (CrossFit methodology)
4. **Acrobatic/Movement Training** — Sprint work, box jumps, jumping variations, lateral agility
5. **Core Work** — Hollow body holds, L-sits, hanging leg raises, plank variations
6. **Swimming** — Pool training for conditioning and mobility

### Signature Exercises
- Ring pull-ups and ring dips
- Kettlebell Turkish get-ups (total-body integration)
- Box jumps + depth jumps (plyometric power)
- Hanging core work (dragon flags, toes-to-bar)
- Dumbbell complexes (press, row, curl, squat circuits)
- Battle rope work (conditioning)
- Bodyweight flow circuits (burpees + push-ups + jumps)

### CrossFit Protocols Used
- **EMOM** (Every Minute On the Minute) — start at top of minute, rest remainder
- **AMRAP** (As Many Rounds As Possible) — timed total-output testing
- Both build the "never gassed" Spider-Man engine

### Training Structure
5-6 days/week during pre-production. Often split morning strength / afternoon movement+skills.

## Synthesis: What These Two Sources Give the Orchestrator

Twight provides the **philosophical foundation**: appearance follows fitness, mind before body, periodised not random, never train to failure, compound movements only.

Ashwell provides the **practical template**: CrossFit-derived EMOM/AMRAP protocols, bodyweight mastery, functional athleticism, the lean-not-bulky target.

Together they define the Spartan-Spider North Star: *train to be capable and the look will follow.*
